Our Story

We believe beauty is a ritual, not a routine.

CELESTÉ was born out of a simple frustration — finding cosmetics that felt luxurious, performed beautifully, and were made without compromise. No harsh chemicals. No animal testing. No half-measures.

We started small, blending formulas in a home studio in the Philippines, obsessing over texture and pigment and finish until every product felt like it belonged in a collection. It still does.

Today, CELESTÉ is worn by women who know what they want — and exactly what they won't settle for.

"Glow from within."

How we got here

The CELESTÉ Timeline

🌱

2022

The Beginning

CELESTÉ launched with three products — a lippie, a gloss, and a blush — all hand-blended and sold through word of mouth in Luzon.

💄

2023

The First Collection

Our first full collection — the Bloom Series — sold out within two weeks of launch. The waitlist proved there was real demand for what we were building.

🌏

2024

Going Online

CELESTÉ launched its digital storefront, making the collection available nationwide with GCash and card payments supported.

✨

2025

Now

A growing range, a loyal community, and a commitment to getting better with every formula — without ever compromising on what we believe in.

What goes in

Key Ingredients

Rose Hip Oil

Rich in vitamins A and C. Nourishes and brightens while adding a silky base to every formula.

Jojoba Wax

A natural emollient that mimics skin's own oils. Keeps lips soft and products long-lasting.

Shea Butter

Deeply moisturizing and anti-inflammatory. The secret behind our buttery, comfortable wear.

Mica Pigments

Ethically sourced mineral pigments that deliver rich, true-to-pan color with buildable intensity.

Vitamin E

An antioxidant that protects and repairs. Every product is fortified with it.

Camellia Extract

A lightweight oil revered in Asian beauty traditions. Adds luminosity without grease.
