Frequently Asked Questions

How long does shipping take?

Standard shipping takes 3–7 business days. Express options are available at checkout for faster delivery.

How can I track my order?

Once your order is dispatched, you'll receive a tracking number via email or SMS to monitor your delivery.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept GCash, Maya, major credit/debit cards, and cash on delivery for select areas.

Do you offer international shipping?

At the moment, we ship within the Philippines only. International shipping is coming soon!

How do I return an item?

Items can be returned within 7 days of delivery if unused and in their original packaging. Contact us to initiate the process.

How long does it take to process a refund?

Refunds are processed within 5–10 business days after the returned item has been inspected and approved.

Are your products cruelty-free?

Yes! All CELESTÉ products are cruelty-free and never tested on animals.

Can I change or cancel my order?

Orders can be modified or cancelled within 24 hours of placement. After that, the order may already be in processing.

What ingredients do you use?

CELESTÉ formulas are free from parabens, sulfates, and synthetic fragrances. We use clean ingredients like rose hip oil, jojoba wax, shea butter, and vitamin E. Full ingredient lists are listed on each product.

Is there a minimum order for free shipping?

Yes — orders over ₱1,500 qualify for free standard shipping anywhere in the Philippines.
