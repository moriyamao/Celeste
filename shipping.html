Shipping & Returns

Shipping

Orders are processed within 2–3 business days. Standard delivery takes 3–7 business days nationwide. Free shipping on orders over ₱1,500.

Returns

Items can be returned within 7 days of delivery if unused and in their original packaging. Contact us to initiate the process.

Refunds

Refunds or exchanges are issued after inspection. Processing takes 5–10 business days. Return shipping costs may apply depending on the reason.

Damaged Items

Received a damaged item? Contact us within 3 days with a photo and we'll arrange a replacement or refund right away.
